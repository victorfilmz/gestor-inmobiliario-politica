Privacy Policy — Gestor Inmobiliario

Español

Last updated: August 17, 2026  ·  Policy version: 1.0  ·  App: Gestor Inmobiliario (Android)  ·  Package: com.victorfilmz.gestorinmobiliario

This policy describes how Gestor Inmobiliario (“the app”) handles the information you store in it. The app is a personal tool for managing properties, rents, bookings, expenses and commissions.

1. Data Controller

The app is developed and published by Victor Enríquez.
Contact email: victorennriquez@gmail.com

For any questions about this policy or about your data, write to the email above.

2. What data the app processes

The app stores only the information you enter manually and that is already on your phone or computer:

  • Property data: address, type, size, price and photos.
  • People data (tenants, owners, clients): name, ID/document number, phone, email and address.
  • Financial data: rents, installments, payments, receipts, expenses, commissions and transaction amounts.
  • Contracts and documents: lease contracts and PDF receipts generated by the app itself.
  • Bookings and maintenance: dates, booking channels (e.g. Airbnb), tasks and reminders.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your phone (Android app): all data is stored in the app's private storage (a local SQLite database inside the app). Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • On your computer (desktop version): the database and documents live in a local folder (Data/) next to the program. It is a desktop program that runs locally.
  • Backups: you can export and import a backup (a .zip file) whenever you want, to move your data between your phone and your computer. You share that copy through the channel of your choice (WhatsApp, email, Drive…).

The app has no servers of its own and no cloud account: your data does not leave your devices except when you share or export it.

4. Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhat it is used for
CameraTake photos of properties inside the app (optional).
Gallery / storagePick photos of properties already saved on your phone.
NotificationsNotify you of the daily digest (overdue installments, contracts about to expire) if you enable it.
InternetOpen or share the public link of the “Tenant Portal” you configure; the app itself does not send data over the internet.

Permissions are requested only when needed (when taking or choosing a photo, when enabling notifications) and can be revoked from Android settings.

5. What the app does NOT do

  • Does not collect usage data or analytics (no Firebase, Google Analytics or similar SDKs).
  • Shows no advertising.
  • Does not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • Has no user accounts or sign-ups; we do not ask for an email or password to use it.
  • Does not send your data to any server automatically.

6. Tenant portal (if you use it)

If you publish or share the portal link of a contract, that link lets the tenant see the summary of their own contract and receipts through the URL you configure (e.g. your server or localhost). You are responsible for publishing and protecting that link, since you control where the app runs and who you share the links with.

7. System backups (Android Auto Backup)

Android may include the app's data in the system backups (Google One / device transfer) of your Google account, as it does with other apps. If you do not want this, you can turn off the app's backup from Settings → Google → Backup, or clear the app's data from Settings → Apps.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete it.
  • You can export and delete all information from More → Data & backups (phone) or Settings → Backup (desktop), including the “start fresh” options.
  • To delete everything on the phone: Android Settings → Apps → Gestor Inmobiliario → Clear data.

9. Security

  • The database lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access.
  • On the desktop you can enable a PIN to protect access to the app (Settings).
  • Backup files you export are not encrypted by the app: store and share them through channels you consider safe.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this document and, when appropriate, notify you from within the app or on the Google Play listing. Continued use of the app after a change implies acceptance of the revised version.

11. Contact

To exercise any right over your data (access, correction, deletion or export) or for any question about this policy: victorennriquez@gmail.com
